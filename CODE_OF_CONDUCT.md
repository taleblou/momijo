# Community Code of Conduct

All participants, contributors and maintainers should keep project discussions respectful and technically constructive. Harassment, targeted insults, discriminatory language, intimidation and disclosure of someone else's private information are not acceptable.

When reporting issues, distinguish reproducible facts from opinions and avoid publishing credentials or private data. Maintainers may moderate issues or PRs and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately through the project's maintainer contacts or a repository private communication channel. Do not use public issues to disclose private information. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent practical; no specific response time is guaranteed.

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, discussions, contribution reviews and affiliated community activities.
